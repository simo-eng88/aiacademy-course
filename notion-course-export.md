## Module 3: Custom GPTs, Research & Literature Review

Arabic: الـ GPTs والبحث العلمي ومراجعة الأدبيات
Subtitle: Custom GPTs · NotebookLM · Research tools · Notion
Date: 5 يونيو 2026
Read time: 13 min
Summary PDF: day3-summary.pdf

### Overview

Day 3 focuses on building custom smart assistants (GPTs), conducting AI-powered academic literature reviews, and organising knowledge in Notion.

### Key Points

- Creating custom GPTs: from GPTs > Explore > Create
- GPT components: Instructions, Knowledge Base, Conversation Starters
- NotebookLM for literature review: upload papers, ask questions and synthesise
- Semantic Scholar: a free academic search engine with semantic (NLP) understanding
- Connected Papers: a visual map of papers related to a key paper
- Research Rabbit: AI-powered paper discovery and collection management
- SciSpace (Typeset): explaining complex papers in simple language
- Consensus: an AI that searches and synthesises results across papers
- Notion as a 'second brain': organising all AI outputs, notes and projects
- The DOI (Digital Object Identifier) â€” how to find and use it

### Takeaways

- Custom GPTs let you create a specialised AI assistant with your knowledge base
- The research pipeline: Semantic Scholar â†’ Connected Papers â†’ Research Rabbit â†’ SciSpace â†’ NotebookLM
- The DOI is the permanent identifier of an academic paper â€” always save it for citations
- Notion is the best tool to organise an AI-powered research workflow

### Tips

- When building a GPT, upload 3â€“5 high-quality reference files to the Knowledge Base
- Write GPT instructions as clear commands and also define what it should NOT do
- Always save the DOI and paper link together
- Use Connected Papers first to map the field, then Research Rabbit to auto-track new work
- Create one Notion database for papers with fields: title, DOI, abstract, status
